Anyone who has a maximum Hearts score can access Sub-Weapons and choose talents from the Sub-Weapon tree.
A character may only have access to one Sub-Weapon at a time, [and may change them on a rest.]

| Ranks | Effect Die | Effect Size        | Effect Range |
| ----- | ---------- | ------------------ | ------------ |
| 1     | d4         | Small (2.5 ft)     | 20 ft.       |
| 2     | d6         | Medium (5 ft)      | 40 ft.       |
| 3     | d8         | Large (10 ft)      | 80 ft.       |
| 4     | d10        | Huge (15 ft)       | 160 ft.      |
| 5     | d12        | Gargantuan (20 ft) | 320 ft.      |

**Dagger**
*Activation:* >
Spend 1 Heart to make a Finesse attack against a target within Effect Range, rolling 2d4 keen damage.
The size of this attack’s damage dice increases with your ranks in Finesse; at 2 ranks, roll 2d6 (instead of 2d4), and so on.

**Holy Water**
*Activation:* >>
Spend 1 Heart to throw this weapon in an area up to the size you can affect with your ranks in Finesse. Make one Finesse attack and compare the test result to the Physical defense of each target of your choice in that area rolling 2d4 keen damage. 
The size of this attack’s damage dice increases with your ranks in Finesse; at 2 ranks, roll 2d6 (instead of 2d4), and so on.

**Cross**
*Activation:* >>
Spend 1 Heart to make a Finesse attack against a target within Effect Range, rolling 2d4 keen damage.
You may immediately spend > and 2 focus to make another Finesse attack against a target within Effect Range (either the same target or a different one), 2d4 keen damage.
The size of this attack’s damage dice increases with your ranks in Finesse; at 2 ranks, roll 2d6 (instead of 2d4), and so on.

**Axe**
*Activation:* >>>
Spend 1 Heart to make a Finesse attack against a target within Effect Range, rolling 3d4 keen damage.
The size of this attack’s damage dice increases with your ranks in Finesse; at 2 ranks, roll 3d6 (instead of 3d4), and so on.

**Stopwatch**
*Activation:* >>
Spend 1 Heart to activate this weapon in an area up to the size you can affect with your ranks in Finesse. Make one Finesse attack and compare the test result to the Spiritual defense of each character of your choice in that area. 
Each character that is hit becomes surprised.

## Sub-Weapon Talents

**Deadly Arsenal**
*Prerequisite:* Sub-Weapons
*Activation:* *
When you hit with a Sub-Weapon attack, you can spend @ to inflict an injury on one target of that attack.

**Long-Distance Warfare**
*Prerequisite:* Sub-Weapons
*Activation:* %
You may treat your Effect Range and Size as if your Finesse were 1 rank higher.

**Devastating Weaponry**
*Prerequisite:* Deadly Arsenal talent
*Activation:* %
When you roll damage for a Sub-Weapon, roll an additional damage die of the same size.

**Last Stand**
*Prerequisite:* Devastating Weaponry talent
*Activation:* #
Once per scene, you can spend 3 focus to use a Sub-Weapon as if you’d instead spent 1 Heart.

**Weaponized Reflex**
*Prerequisite:* Devastating Weaponry talent
*Activation:* -
Spend 2 focus to gain >, which you can only use to activate your Sub-Weapons.
